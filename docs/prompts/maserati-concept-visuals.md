# Maserati-inspired GT concept visual prompts

These prompts document the three original AI-generated reference images used on `/demo/maserati`. They describe an unbranded concept, not an official Maserati vehicle or parts catalogue. The interactive model remains procedural Three.js geometry and is not reconstructed CAD.

## 1. Exterior hero — `hero.webp`

> Create one premium, photorealistic 3D automotive studio render of an original deep metallic blue Italian grand touring coupe concept inspired by Maserati design language. Front three-quarter view, full vehicle visible, elegant long hood, low wide stance, sculpted side body, refined vertical-slat grille with a simple unbranded oval badge (no readable logos or text), realistic headlights and wheels, subtle studio reflections, dark charcoal seamless background, soft cinematic key light and rim light, high-end CGI, realistic materials, clean professional automotive product visualization. No people, no watermark, no text, no collage, no extra vehicles. Landscape 16:9 composition.

## 2. Multi-angle turntable reference — `turntable.webp`

> Using the supplied blue luxury GT coupe as the exact visual reference, create a clean 360-degree automotive turntable contact sheet showing the SAME car consistently from eight evenly spaced viewpoints around it: front, front three-quarter, side, rear three-quarter, rear, opposite rear three-quarter, opposite side, opposite front three-quarter. Arrange as one horizontal strip of eight equally sized panels, full car completely visible in every panel, consistent vehicle proportions, paint, wheels, lighting, camera height, and dark seamless studio background. Maintain the same original unbranded design. Do not add logos, text, people, extra cars, or change vehicle styling. Professional high-end 3D visualization reference sheet.

## 3. Major assembly exploded overview — `exploded-parts.webp`

> Create one premium technical 3D exploded-view parts overview for the same deep-blue Italian luxury GT coupe in the reference. Keep the design visually consistent with the reference but unbranded. Show the car's major serviceable assemblies separated in an orderly, clear exploded layout around a central car chassis: outer body panels (hood, front bumper, doors, fenders, roof, trunk lid), V8 engine assembly and intake, transmission, exhaust, front and rear subframes, steering rack, suspension arms and dampers, four wheels with brake discs/calipers, radiator/cooling pack, headlights and taillights, seats, dashboard, steering wheel, doors interior trim, battery and a few electronic modules. Every part should be visually distinct and properly proportioned, with subtle thin guide lines tying parts to their mounting locations. Dark charcoal studio background, crisp studio lighting, professional automotive engineering visualization, landscape 16:9. No labels, no text, no logos, no people, no extra cars. This is an illustrative concept breakdown, not a factory service manual or exact OEM parts diagram.

## Accuracy and use

- The pictures are visual concept references only. Image generation can introduce inconsistencies between views and is not photogrammetry.
- The GLB download and interactive viewer use separately authored procedural geometry. They are not measured from these pictures and are not official OEM CAD.
- Do not use the visuals to identify exact part numbers, model-year fitment, specifications, or repair procedures.
- The turntable prompt asked for eight panels in one strip; the generated `turntable.webp` actually shows twelve views in two rows of six, and details can differ between panels.
- The hero render echoes recognisable Maserati styling cues (oval vertical-slat grille, three side vents). It is labelled "Maserati-inspired" and every image carries an "AI concept art · not OEM" badge, but have the name, the badge-like grille emblem and the styling reviewed for trademark and trade dress before any public use.
