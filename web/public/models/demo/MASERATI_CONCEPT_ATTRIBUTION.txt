Maserati-inspired GT coupe concept

This model was generated for this project from original Three.js procedural geometry.
It contains no third-party vehicle mesh or texture files.

It is an illustrative design concept, not an exact Maserati production vehicle, an
OEM scan, or a source of genuine manufacturer parts, measurements, specifications,
or fitment information. Maserati is not affiliated with or endorsing this project.

The downloadable GLB is generated by scripts/export_maserati_concept_glb.mjs from
web/lib/maseratiConcept.ts.
